Policy

We believe great working relationships start with clarity. This policy covers everything you need to know about how sessions are structured, what's included, and what happens in edge cases — so there are zero surprises on shoot day.

01 — Session Structure

What "Session Time" Means

Your booked session time is the total time our team is at your location — from arrival to packdown. It is not pure shooting time.

All sessions include professional setup and packdown, which is handled entirely by our team. You don't need to do anything — just be ready when we tell you the studio is live.

Session Setup & Packdown Production Time
3-Hour Session ~90 minutes ~1.5 hours
5-Hour Session ~90 minutes ~3.5 hours
8-Hour Session (Full Day) ~90–120 minutes ~6 hours

Setup and packdown time is non-negotiable — it's how we guarantee broadcast-quality results every session.

If you'd like more pure production time, we recommend upgrading to the next session length.

02 — Booking & Payment

Confirming Your Session

A session is only confirmed once we receive a 50% deposit. Until payment is received, your date and time slot remains unconfirmed and may be offered to other clients.

  • Deposit: 50% of total session price, paid at booking
  • Remaining balance: Due on the day of the session, before we begin
  • Payment methods: Bank transfer (BCA/Mandiri), GoPay, or cash (IDR)
  • Invoice: Sent via WhatsApp upon booking confirmation
03 — Cancellation & Rescheduling

Changes to Your Booking

We understand plans change. Here's our cancellation and rescheduling policy:

Notice Given Deposit Rescheduling
7+ days before Full refund Free, subject to availability
3–6 days before 50% refund Free, subject to availability
Less than 3 days Non-refundable Rescheduling fee applies
No-show / same day Non-refundable Full session fee applies

To cancel or reschedule, contact us via WhatsApp. All changes must be confirmed in writing.

04 — Unused Session Time

What Happens If You Finish Early

If your recording wraps before the session ends, unused time cannot be transferred or refunded. When you book a session, we reserve that entire time block exclusively for you — turning away other clients for that slot.

Unused time is non-transferable and non-refundable.

Remaining on-site time can still be used: reviewing footage together, discussing edits, reshooting scenes, or planning your next session.

05 — Overtime

Running Beyond Your Session

We will always give you a 15-minute heads-up before your session ends so you can decide whether to continue.

Overtime Duration Charge
1–60 minutes IDR 600,000 (1 full hour)
61–90 minutes IDR 900,000 (1.5 hours)
91–120 minutes IDR 1,200,000 (2 hours)
  • Overtime is charged in 30-minute increments after the first hour
  • Minimum overtime charge is 1 full hour
  • Overtime is subject to team availability — not always possible to extend
  • Overtime payment is due on the day, in addition to your session balance
06 — Night Surcharge

Evening Sessions After 18.00

A flat surcharge of IDR 400,000 applies to any session that continues past 18.00 WIB. This covers the additional logistics and time commitment for our team for evening work.

Night surcharge: IDR 400,000 flat — applies when any part of your session runs past 18.00.

This is always disclosed at booking. It will never be added as a surprise at the end of a session.

07 — Lunch Break

Breaks During Long Sessions

For sessions of 8 hours (Full Day), a 30-minute lunch break is included and does not count toward production time. This break is for both the client and crew.

For sessions of 5 hours or less, no mandatory break is required. If a break is requested mid-session by the client, it will be counted as session time.

08 — Client Responsibilities

What We Need From You

  • Location access: Ensure our team can access the shoot location on arrival — including parking, building access, and any security requirements
  • Location readiness: The shoot space should be cleared and reasonably ready before we arrive
  • Punctuality: If the client is late, the session start time does not shift — session time begins at the agreed start time
  • Content preparation: Have your scripts, talking points, or content plan ready before the session. Time spent on content prep during the session counts as session time
  • Internet (if needed): If streaming or live output is required, the client is responsible for providing a stable internet connection
09 — Travel & Location

Where We Operate

We cover Canggu, Seminyak, Kuta, Legian, Denpasar, Sanur, Ubud, and Uluwatu as standard service areas with no travel surcharge.

Locations outside these areas are available upon request. A travel surcharge will be quoted at booking based on distance and logistics.

  • Travel surcharge is always disclosed before booking confirmation
  • Remote or difficult-access locations may require additional time added to the session
10 — Agreement

By Booking, You Agree

By paying the deposit and confirming a session with Nomad Studio Bali, you confirm that you have read and agree to this Booking Policy in full.

We send a copy of this policy with every booking confirmation. If you have any questions before booking, we're happy to walk through anything via WhatsApp.
